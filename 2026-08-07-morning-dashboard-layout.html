Good morning. Two days ago you ended on an edge: staying present with conceptual content — “writing on water,” letting concepts appear vividly and leave no trace — instead of always resolving thoughts down to sensation.

What’s alive this morning?

Slept well. There’s some hum of excitement about the day that I can feel pulling at me already. I want to keep working with the radiant face of the view — can excitement itself be transparent? I keep treating it as a threat to presence rather than an expression of it.

Asked Rigdzin notebook
“How should a practitioner relate to excitement or eagerness during practice — is it an obstacle to rest, or can it be included as radiance?”
Sit

The notebook points at the same instruction from a new angle: excitement is “movement arising within stillness” — not the opposite of rest but its display. The fault isn’t the arising, it’s the chasing. “Neither suppress nor pursue: left to itself, movement is self-liberating.”

A frame for this sit: when excitement rises, don’t guard against it and don’t ride it. Let it be weather.

That frame worked better than I expected. Excitement rose maybe a dozen times, and twice it genuinely liberated on its own — it crested and dissolved and left a kind of shimmer rather than a hangover. The other times I caught myself already three thoughts deep into planning the day. The tell is a leaning-forward feeling in the chest before any content shows up. Overall one of the more balanced sits recently. Feels complete.
